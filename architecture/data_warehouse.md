## Data Warehouse

## Goals of Data Warehousing and Business Intelligence

* The DW/BI system must make information easily accesible
* The DW/BI system must present infromation consistently
* The DW/BI system must adapt to change
* The DW/BI system must present information in a timely way
* The DW/BI system must be a secure bastion that protects the infromation assets
* The DW/BI system must serve as the authoriative and trustworthy foundation for improved decision making
* The business community must accept the DW/BI ssytem to deem it successful
