- The Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) is a unique global identifier for legal entities participating in financial transactions

- 4 digits
     - LEI issuer
     - Local Operating Units or LOUs
- 14 digits
    - eintity identifier
    - LEI issuers assign the number
- 2 digits
    - verification ID
